Privacy Policy

How Damini Estate collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

Last updated: 16 September 2026

Damini Real Estate L.L.C ("Damini Estate", "we", "us") operates the website daminiestate.ae. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal data transparently and in line with applicable UAE data protection law.

What we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, email, phone number, and the contents of any enquiry, chat message or newsletter sign-up you send us.
  • Technical information: your IP address, approximate location (country/city), device and browser type, and referring page, used to operate, secure and measure the site.
  • Marketing attribution: if you arrive via a campaign, we record the campaign parameters (e.g. UTM tags) and ad click identifiers attached to your visit.
  • Usage data: the pages you view, how you scroll and click, time on page, and recordings of your session, as described under Cookies & tracking.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiries and provide our real estate services.
  • To send you information you requested, such as our newsletter (you can unsubscribe at any time).
  • To operate, secure, and improve our website.
  • To comply with our legal and regulatory obligations as a RERA-licensed brokerage.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with trusted service providers who help us operate, for example our CRM (GoHighLevel), website analytics (Yandex Metrica and our website provider), email delivery, and hosting (Cloudflare), and only to the extent needed to provide the service. These providers are bound to protect your data. We may also disclose information where required by law.

Cookies & tracking

We use cookies and similar browser storage for these purposes:

  • Essential: remembering settings such as your language.
  • Yandex Metrica: records page views, clicks, scrolling and recordings of your session, and sets identifiers for your browser. Text you type into our enquiry, valuation and newsletter forms is masked in the recordings. Yandex may process this data outside the UAE.
  • First-party analytics: a script run by our website provider records the pages you view, how far you scroll, how long you stay, the links you click to leave the site, call or email us, and the campaign and ad that brought you here (remembered in your browser for up to 365 days). It also creates an identifier from characteristics of your browser and device. If you type an email address into one of our forms, it records that address even if you do not submit the form, so your visit can be linked to an enquiry.
  • CRM page tracking: our CRM records the pages you visit and the forms you submit.
  • Live chat: the chat widget stores a session so a conversation continues across pages. Messages you send go to our CRM.

If your browser sends a "Do Not Track" or Global Privacy Control signal, Yandex Metrica and the CRM page tracking do not load. The first-party analytics and the live chat still run.

Data retention

We keep your information only as long as necessary for the purposes above, or as required by law, after which it is deleted or anonymised.
